FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Everything people want to know before booking a cleaning.

How much does it cost?

Cleaning prices depend on your home's size, current condition, and the type of service you need. Send us your details and we'll provide a clear, personalized quote. New clients choosing regular cleaning receive 10% off their first regular service. Deep cleaning is quoted at the standard rate.

Who's actually coming into my home?

A cleaner from the KD Cleaning Services team, and we keep the same person on your home whenever the schedule allows, so you're not meeting someone new every visit.

What's the difference between a deep clean and a regular clean?

A deep clean gets everything, including what never gets touched week to week: inside appliances, baseboards, grout, behind furniture. A regular clean keeps that result going. If your home hasn't had a deep clean recently, start there.

Do I need to be home?

Up to you. Plenty of clients let us in and go about their day. Others prefer to stay. Whatever makes you comfortable works for us.

Do you bring your own supplies?

Yes, everything. If you'd rather we use specific products in your home, leave them out and we'll use yours.

I have pets. Is that a problem?

Not at all. Just tell us in advance so we know who we're meeting and whether any doors need to stay closed.

How do I pay?

After the clean is finished and you've seen the result. Never before.

How does the 10% off work?

The discount applies to your first regular cleaning with KD Cleaning Services. It does not apply to deep cleaning. No promotional code is needed — select Regular Cleaning in the quote form and the discount will be included in your price.

FAQ

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How much do your house cleaning services cost in Tampa?

Our pricing is based on the size of your space (square footage), the number of bedrooms and bathrooms, and the condition of the property.

What areas around Tampa do you serve?

We serve residential and commercial clients across the entire Tampa Bay area, covering Hillsborough, Pinellas, and Pasco counties — including Tampa, St. Petersburg, Clearwater, Largo, Brandon, Riverview, Plant City, Palm Harbor, Wesley Chapel, and many more. Don't see your city? Send us your ZIP code and we'll confirm right away.

Do I need to provide my own cleaning supplies or equipment?

No. KD Cleaning Services brings all standard professional cleaning products, microfibers, vacuums, and specialized equipment needed to complete the job.

How long does a typical deep cleaning service take?

Duration depends on the size and initial condition of the property. Most residential deep cleanings take between 3 and 6 hours. Regular recurring cleanings generally take between 2 and 4 hours.

Do I need to be home while you clean?

That is entirely up to you. Many of our clients provide key access, garage codes, or entry instructions if they are at work or running errands during the cleaning window. We handle all properties with care and security.

How do recurring cleanings work?

We offer weekly, bi-weekly, and monthly recurring cleaning schedules. Once we complete an initial deep clean or standard session, we lock in your preferred day and time block for ongoing maintenance.
